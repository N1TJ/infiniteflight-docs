# 7D.4 Observing



### 7D.4.1

Supervisors have the ability to observe active frequencies on the Expert Server. Observing a frequency **should**{.red} be used for the following purposes: 

* Observing controllers in the Checkride phase to provide feedback, ensure procedural compliance, and determine readiness for completion of their Checkride

* Observing event/high-volume HUB airports where marginal performance has been reported

* Observe controllers that have requested feedback 

  > Supervisors should make every effort to log notes capturing observations and feedback provided to controller

### 7D.4.2

The purpose of the Observer mode is to provide additional operational oversight and a means to determine rank readiness for Checkride controllers. Supervisors should exercise good judgement when using the Observer mode and select appropriate situations to observe in an effort to encourage controller independence and confidence.

> Observing should not be used so disproportionately that controllers are discouraged from opening a frequency



### 7D.4.3

Complete the following steps to observe a frequency: 



Step 1

: Launch the Infinite Flight app and select ATC Mode



Step 2

: Choose an airport on the Expert Server with an active controller



Step 3

: Select the frequencies you wish to observe

> green boxes and text denote a staffed frequency that may be observed



Step 4

: Select "Observe" to launch ATC session in observer mode



Step 5

: Select "Follow" to mirror map view and panning from active controller
