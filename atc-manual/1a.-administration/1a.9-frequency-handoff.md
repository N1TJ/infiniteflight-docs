# 1A.9 Frequency Handoff



### 1A.9.1

Controllers can use the frequency handoff function to request relief from available controllers for all frequencies under their control, some frequencies under their control, or to request control of a frequency from another active controller. 

### 1A.9.2

Controllers **should**{red} use the automated means of conducting a handoff in-app, however controllers **may**{red} still use the traditional relief tags on Discord to transfer control of a frequency.

### 1A.9.3

Individual controllers **must**{red} comply with and meet the minimum control times for their selected frequency despite utilizing the handoff function to ensure continuous frequency activity.



### 1A.9.4

Regardless of which handoff method is used, controllers **must**{red} provide relieving controller with any pertinent frequency information, such as last ATIS code, runways in use, airport/airspace configuration, pending tasks, and any other relevant information.

### 1A.9.5

While actively controlling, complete the following steps to initiate a handoff to another controller(s):

Step 1

: Select the main menu in the bottom right 



Step 2

: On the airport information card, select "Handoff"



Step 3

: Select some or all frequencies to initiate a handoff with 

> selecting individual frequencies to handoff will allow another controller to manage an individual frequency within your selected facility and will seamlessly drop your control of that frequency without interruption



Step 4

: Select #controllers or type an individual controller username 

> #controllers will send an automated message in the #controller channel on Discord. Once claimed, the relieving controller will have 5 mintutes to launch Infinite Flight and staff the claimed frequency



Step 5

: Select "Post" to send handoff request to #controllers channel or individual user

_Note: Handoffs to specific controllers **should**{red} not be used to accomplish private frequency reservations. To the max extent possible, communicate handoffs in a public setting to ensure fair opportunities for all IFATC members_

Step 6

: To cancel the handoff request, select "Cancel" from the ATC map screen or on the main menu info card where the handoff was initiated. 

> Automated messages will be sent in #controllers to indicate a cancelled request or frequency closure prior to completing a successful handoff

Step 7 

: Relieved controller should observe relieving controller momentarily to ensure a smooth handoff and provide answers to any questions 



### 1A.9.6

When starting a new ATC session, complete the following steps to request a handoff from an active controller:



Step 1

: In the ATC mode, select an airport with active control frequencies

> Active frequencies are represented by the green frequency box and text



Step 2

: Select some or all the active frequencies that you wish to take over 



Step 3

: Select "Control" to launch session





Step 4

: Observe active controller while configuring your map and settings

> Relieving controller should select "Follow" to mirror active controller's view. Observe long enough to note airport/airspace configuration, flow, pending/completed tasks, and other relevant information to ensure a smooth transition



Step 5

: Upon acceptance of the handoff from the active controller, you will resume control of the frequency and have immediate control over all aircraft

_Note: A handoff request may be denied by the active controller or cancelled by the relieving controller by selecting "Cancel". Both actions will terminate the session and return to the main menu._
