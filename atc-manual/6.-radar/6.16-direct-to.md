# 6.16 Direct-To 

 

### 6.16.1 

The direct-to function allows you to send an aircraft direct to a fix, NAVAID or airport on their filed flight plan. An aircraft's flight plan is visible by tapping their aircraft icon on the map. To send an aircraft direct to a fix, use the drag and vector tool to hover over the desired fix or select from the "vectoring instructions" menu for a particular aircraft.



### 6.16.2

Controllers **may**{.red} use the direct-to function to accomplish the following: 

- sequence or space arriving and departing aircraft
- shortcut aircraft on their flight plan
- direct aircraft to the initial approach fix (IAF) for an approach
- position aircraft for an approach, such as a visual approach



### 6.16.3    

Controllers **must**{.red} only issue direct-to clearances to a fix, NAVAID, or airport on an aircraft's flight plan within their area of jurisdiction. Controllers **should**{.red} consider the extensive flight planning required by pilots and should minimize interference with realistic routing. (e.g. Clearing an aircraft direct destination 3,000 miles away removes carefully planned routing and fuel considerations)
